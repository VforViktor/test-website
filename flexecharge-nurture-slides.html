Sales Enablement — Why Frequency Matters 02 / 03

Frequency Builds Trust

Audience penetration above 100% means decision-makers have seen Flexecharge multiple times. Your SDRs aren't cold-calling — they're following up.

New BESS+HPC+OIL campaign launched 12 March, resetting audience frequency to zero. Average dropped from 1.87× to 1.30× — audiences are still warming up. Full effect visible in April.

Audience Penetration — Month-over-Month

Why the dip? When a campaign is rebuilt, frequency and penetration reset to zero for new audiences. The 23 conversions in March came despite only a half-month of warm-up — April will show the full compound effect of the new setup.

What this means for Sales

  • Fewer site visits (107 vs 338) means fewer retargeted leads this month — but the 23 who did convert came through a full awareness + retargeting sequence.
  • New audiences added on 12 March need 4–6 weeks before they're fully warm. April pipeline should be significantly stronger.
  • The TF-TL viral post (105K reach) seeded brand awareness organically — unpaid impressions that prime future paid retargeting.
  • CPC rising to ~$10 signals new, cold audiences — expected during a campaign reset. Cost will drop as frequency builds.
